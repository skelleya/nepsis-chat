# Nepsis Chat 0.2.4

Floating voice cameras while browsing text channels and DMs.

## Highlights

- **Voice PiP overlay** — While connected to voice but viewing a text channel, DM, or Friends, camera tiles for people with video (speaking highlighted) float in a corner.
- **Moveable** — Drag the panel; it snaps to any corner (top-left, top-right, bottom-left, bottom-right). Choice is remembered.
- **Return to voice** — Click a tile or Open to jump back to the voice channel UI.

## Notes

- Hidden while the full VoiceView is open, and while a DM call overlay is active.
- Up to four camera tiles; speaking users are sorted first.
